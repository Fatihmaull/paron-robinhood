# Paron: quick industry scan (design v1)

Paron Product Designer, Fri 9 Oct 2026, ~12:05 WIB. About 30 minutes of desk research. Every finding links to a source. "Paron takes" says what we copy or avoid. Nothing here is a claim about Paron's partners: we have none.

| # | Finding (sourced) | Paron takes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Robinhood's 2024 identity** is black, white and neutrals plus "Robin Neon", an electric yellow-green (`#CCFF00`), with thin lines on black. Sources: [Porto Rocha case study](https://portorocha.com/robinhood), [Robinhood announcement via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ROBINHOOD-MARKETS-INC-125228571/news/Robinhood-Markets-Introducing-a-New-Visual-Identity-Reflecting-Robinhood-s-Growth-and-Vision-for-48045406/), [BrandColorCode](https://www.brandcolorcode.com/robinhood). | **Hard avoid.** No lime/neon yellow-green as brand or accent. The L2 scaffold (PR #2 `web/app/globals.css`) uses `--accent: #d6ff4a` on green-tinted black, which reads as Robin Neon. v1 tokens replace it with a forge-ember accent on cool graphite. Buy/bid green stays a calm teal-green and is only used for bids/positive deltas. |
| 2 | **Hyperliquid's trade screen** has four regions: market header, chart, order book, order panel on the right, with positions/history in a bottom strip. Source: [perpdexguide: the Hyperliquid screen](https://perpdexguide.com/hyperliquid/trading-interface/). | S3 Series page keeps the 06 three-column grid (book · tape · buy/bond) under a full-width series header. No chart is needed for S0. Header = identity + price + bond terms, the same role as a market header. |
| 3 | Hyperliquid is praised for **restraint**: a few core modules, no campaign banners or marketing pop-ups competing for attention, unlike 10+ module CEX screens. Source: [OneKey blog: Hyperliquid vs CEX UX](https://onekey.so/blog/ecosystem/hyperliquid-cex-ux-comparison/). | Institutional = calm. One accent, no gradients or glow in data areas, no confetti. Banners only for the real system states in 06 §1.6 (network, indexer, mock, paused). |
| 4 | **Bloomberg Terminal** runs CVD color schemes (≈20,000 users with color-vision deficiency). The Deuteranopia scheme uses **blue for up / red for down** and keeps amber for non-semantic information. Source: [Bloomberg UX: Designing the Terminal for color accessibility](https://www.bloomberg.com/ux/2021/10/14/designing-the-terminal-for-color-accessibility/). | Never encode buy/sell or status **only** by color. Every pill carries its word ("OK", "THIN", "Deadline missed"), bids/asks carry side labels, and the tape has a Type column. Bid green and ask red are picked to differ in lightness too. Optional CVD swap (bid → blue) is a one-token change. |
| 5 | **Centrifuge app principle "Overview first, detail on demand"**: every entity has a summary; details open in their own view. Also "one app for investors and issuers". Source: [Centrifuge forum: Enter the new Centrifuge app](https://gov.centrifuge.io/t/enter-the-new-centrifuge-app/4072). | S1 Market row = summary (price, bond, coverage, record); S3 = detail. Provider console and holder portfolio share one shell and one token set, as 06 already specifies. |
| 6 | Centrifuge pool pages open with a **summary section**, then token list, then history graphs with **CSV download** for each graph. Source: [Centrifuge forum: The new pool details view](https://gov.centrifuge.io/t/the-new-pool-details-view/4189). | S3 header is the summary block (symbol, GPU, window, primary price, default compensation). Export CSV lives on S4/V-STMT as in 06 §7. |
| 7 | Centrifuge cut pool creation to **three steps** for managers. Source: [Centrifuge blog: pool creation update](https://centrifuge.io/blog/app-update-pool-creation). | Confirms the S2 "forge a series" 3-step wizard (Capacity → Terms → Bond & launch) with a persistent preview card; the stepper is a first-class component. |
| 8 | **Ondo's trust page** leads with collateral facts: full backing plus over-collateralization, a named third-party verification agent, daily attestations, audits. Source: [Ondo docs: Trust & Transparency](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/trust-and-transparency). | For RWA, trust is the product. The bond health bar, "Default compensation $4.50 per CU (fixed)", "Verified by Paron demo verifier" and the attestation drawer get top visual priority, not footnotes. Never imply third-party verification we don't have (verifier is "Paron demo verifier", D-04). |
| 9 | **SF Compute's GPU order book** prices H100 time per exact **delivery window**, with best bid/ask per window. Prices are decimal strings per node-hour, and orders are **immediate-or-cancel by default** unless "allow standing". Source: [SF Compute docs: Orderbook](https://docs.sfcompute.com/preview/orderbook), [Orders](https://docs.sfcompute.com/preview/orders). | Compute markets already treat the window as part of the instrument. Paron puts the window in the symbol (`CU-JKT-H100-2610`) and shows "Redeemable 1–31 Oct 2026" next to the price. Prices always show the unit ("$3.20/CU"). The IOC checkbox copy stays explicit (06 §4.4). |
| 10 | **Vast.ai** shows a host **reliability score** on offer cards and lets you filter offers by it. Source: [Vast.ai docs: search interface](https://vast.ai/docs/instance-rental/search-interface). | Provider track record belongs in the main table: S1 "Record" column (delivered / defaulted / declined) in mono, with a defaulted count > 0 in danger color. |
| 11 | `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` gives every digit the same width so columns of numbers line up; it only works if the font has tabular figures. Source: [MDN: font-variant-numeric](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/font-variant-numeric). | Prices, sizes, countdowns and addresses use **IBM Plex Mono** (tabular by design); numbers inside sans text use Plex Sans with `tnum`. Numeric table cells are right-aligned. |
| 12 | **WCAG 2.2**: body text needs ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥ 3:1 (SC 1.4.3), and UI component boundaries/states ≥ 3:1 (SC 1.4.11). Sources: [W3C Understanding SC 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [SC 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). | Every text token is checked with `scripts/contrast.py`; results are in `contrast-report.md`. Juries will view on projectors and phones, so we aim for ≥ 7:1 on primary text. |

## What Paron is NOT (from the scan)
- Not a meme launchpad: no neon, no rocket/flame emoji, no bonding-curve visuals. pump.fun is only an analogy for listing speed.
- Not a Robinhood look-alike: no lime, no feather, no thin-line-on-black hero style, no serif headline + neon combo.
- Not a perps venue: leverage only appears as the "Coming soon" tab with the exact copy from 06 §0.5.
